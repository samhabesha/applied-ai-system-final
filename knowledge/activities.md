# Activity notes (custom knowledge base)

Listeners often describe *what they are doing* instead of a genre. These notes
translate activities into a starting profile. `mood`, `energy` and `acoustic`
are defaults that the listener's own words can override.

## workout
aliases: gym, lifting, running, run, cardio, training, exercise
mood: intense
energy: 0.9
acoustic: no
High tempo and strong beats keep pace during exercise.

## study
aliases: studying, homework, reading, coding, work, exam
mood: focused
energy: 0.35
acoustic: yes
Low-distraction music without loud vocals helps concentration.

## sleep
aliases: bedtime, fall asleep, nap, insomnia
mood: peaceful
energy: 0.15
acoustic: yes
Very soft, slow, acoustic sound.

## party
aliases: dance, dancing, celebration, pregame, club night
mood: happy
energy: 0.85
acoustic: no
Danceable, bright songs everyone knows how to move to.

## road trip
