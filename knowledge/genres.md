# Genre notes (custom knowledge base)

Each section is one retrievable chunk. `family` groups related genres so the
recommender can give partial credit when the exact genre is not in the catalog.

## pop
family: pop
aliases: pop music, top 40, mainstream
energy: 0.75
Bright, catchy, hook-driven songs built for wide appeal. Pop listeners usually want upbeat tempos and clear vocals.

## indie pop
family: pop
aliases: indie, bedroom pop
energy: 0.7
Pop songwriting with a lighter, more homemade sound. Fans of pop often enjoy it even though the label differs.

## k-pop
family: pop
aliases: kpop, korean pop, j-pop, jpop
energy: 0.8
Highly produced, dance-ready pop from Korea (j-pop is the Japanese cousin). Not in our catalog, so the closest matches are pop and indie pop songs.

## synthwave
family: electronic
aliases: retrowave, outrun, 80s synth
energy: 0.7
Retro 1980s synthesizers and drum machines. Moody, cinematic and good for night drives.

## edm
family: electronic
aliases: electronic, edm music, house, techno, dubstep, rave, club
energy: 0.9
Electronic dance music: big drops, steady four-on-the-floor beats, made for clubs and workouts.

## lofi
family: chill
aliases: lo-fi, lofi hip hop, chillhop, beats to study to
energy: 0.4
Soft, dusty beats with warm samples. The classic background for studying, coding and focus.

## ambient
family: chill
aliases: soundscape, drone, meditation music
energy: 0.25
Slow, textured sound with little or no beat. Good for sleep, meditation and deep focus.

## jazz
family: soul
aliases: smooth jazz, bebop, swing
energy: 0.4
Improvised, warm and acoustic. Fits coffee shops, dinner and relaxed evenings.

## blues
family: soul
aliases: delta blues, blues rock
energy: 0.35
Emotional, guitar-led music about hard times. Usually slow and sad, sometimes gritty.

## r&b
family: soul
aliases: rnb, rhythm and blues, soul, neo soul
energy: 0.5
Smooth vocals and groove. Romantic and late-night.

## rock
family: rock
aliases: rock music, alternative, alt rock, grunge, punk
energy: 0.85
Guitar, bass and drums with a strong drive. Intense rock suits workouts and loud moods.

## metal
family: rock
aliases: heavy metal, hard rock, thrash
energy: 0.95
The heaviest corner of rock: distorted guitars, fast drums, aggressive energy. If a listener says "nothing too heavy", avoid it.

## hip hop
family: hip hop
aliases: hip-hop, rap, trap, drill
energy: 0.75
Rhythm and rhymes over beats. Often confident and good for workouts or commuting.

## classical
family: classical
aliases: orchestral, piano, symphony, soundtrack, instrumental
energy: 0.2
Orchestral or solo instrumental music. Calm, acoustic and great for studying or sleep.

## country
family: roots
aliases: americana, bluegrass
energy: 0.55
Storytelling with guitars and twang. Often nostalgic, made for road trips.

## folk
family: roots
aliases: singer-songwriter, acoustic folk, indie folk
energy: 0.3
Acoustic, lyrical and intimate. Fits rainy days and quiet reflection.

## latin
family: latin
aliases: reggaeton, salsa, bachata, latin pop, afrobeats
energy: 0.85
Rhythmic, danceable and joyful. Great for parties and summer.

## reggae
family: latin
aliases: dancehall, roots reggae, ska
energy: 0.5
Laid-back offbeat rhythm from Jamaica. Relaxed, sunny and easygoing.
